THE GUIDE

The Guía del Corredor 2026 (49 pages, Spanish only, published 6 November) is the race-week handbook for all seven CBT distances. This page keeps the 102K content, ordered the way race week unfolds. Rules, kit and penalties live on 102K Regs; the route itself on Course.

  • The 102K carries UTMB World Series Qualifier status (Qualifier 100K (M)), ITRA 5 points and ITRA National League status — new information, not in the regs.
  • Everything happens at Plaza Unión Europea, Finestrat (map): bibs, start, finish, showers nearby, expo, dining hall.

BEFORE THE START

Enter the start corral (“corralito”) in good time — the GPS tracker is issued at the corral entrance and has to be fitted there.

  • GPS tracker (102K and 66K only): switch it on at least 1 hour before the start — hold the power button until the red light comes on and it vibrates (no sharp objects on the button). It has GPS, battery and signal LEDs, a QR code to check your bib and share your race, and an SOS button: hold 5 seconds to signal the organisers. Keep it protected from sweat (sealed bag). Switch it off at least 5 minutes after you finish and return it at the finish.
  • Timing chip on the pack: fix it to the front of your vest, on one strap, tied firmly with the cable tie; visible and uncovered for the whole race — never inside the pack. Losing it is a DSQ (regs).
  • Drop bag (“bolsa de vida”): hand it in at the area designated as you pass the start control. Fix the bib-number label to one strap only (a top handle), cable tie through the hole, label hanging free — not flattened, folded, or across both straps, which can snap it. Nothing may stick out of the bag. The guide doesn't name the 102K life-bag point; the regs say Confrides, 58.5 km.
  • Referees (FEMECV) will check the rules: stay on the course, carry the kit, no litter, mark your gels and bars, bib visible on your front. The kit list in the guide matches the regs page, bar one food-reserve wording difference (see the callout below).
  • The start serves coffee and hot chocolate only — no water. Your 1 litre must already be full.

SHARED COURSE WITH THE 66K

The UTCB 66K starts Saturday 07:00 and runs the second half of the 102K course: its later controls are the same Font de Partagat, Font del Alemany, Sella and Mas del Oficial, and its last-runner times there (15:30, 18:00, 19:00, 22:00) equal the 102K cut-offs.

  • Expect 66K traffic on the trail and at aid stations from roughly Font de Partagat (67.4 km) onward — the 66K leader is through there around 10:30.
  • The MCB 41K (starts Sat 08:30) also passes through Mas del Oficial.
  • Shared finish deadline: the line closes for both 102K and 66K at 23:00 Saturday.

LIVE TRACKING & RESULTS

  • Live classification: the Cruzando la Meta Live app (Google Play and App Store) shows real-time positions as runners pass the control points, all distances.
  • GPS map (Track the Race): the guide's “MAPA SEGUIMIENTO GTCB” button carries no hyperlink — only the 66K map is linked, at tracktherace.com/…-utcb-66k/race. The 102K URL presumably follows the same pattern ending …-gtcb-102k/race, but the guide does not confirm it.
  • No 102K Komoot event: Komoot group live tracking is listed for the other distances only. For the 102K, tracking is the carried device.

FINISH, SHOWERS, PRIZES & PHOTOS

  • At the line: return the GPS tracker, switching it off at least 5 minutes after finishing. The finish station serves the final meal plus coffee, fruit, sandwiches and more; the venue has the dining hall, recovery/physio and left luggage.
  • Showers: map link — the guide gives no building name; the regs say the Finestrat sports hall.
  • Prize giving: Sunday 11:30 for the 102K and 66K, first three in every category. Prize-winners should be at the awards area 10 minutes early; per the regs, skipping the ceremony renounces the trophy.
  • Photos: four official photographers publish to Flickr albums; Mountains Eye posts to social media in near real time; new this year, Imagen Media shoots start to finish with facial-recognition search at imagemedia.fotop.com (a separate service from the organisers').

NOT IN THE GUIDE — ASK AT PICKUP

The guide is silent on all of these. Bib pickup is the natural place to get answers:

  • ID requirement for bib collection.
  • The emergency phone number. The kit list requires a phone “with the number provided”, but the number isn't printed anywhere — save it from the bib pack or briefing.
  • A race briefing time — none is listed.
  • Shuttles or transport, including how the ride back from a withdrawal point actually works.
  • Where and when kit is checked — only “enter the corral in good time” and that FEMECV referees will check compliance.
  • Post-race drop-bag return times (the regs say Saturday until the finish closes, or Sunday 10:00–14:00).
  • Street addresses for parking and showers (map links only), weather contingency routes, and a Wikiloc track link for the 102K — every other distance gets one.
  • Which partner shops do bib pre-collection, and on what dates.

WHERE THE GUIDE DISAGREES WITH OUR PAGES

Benimantell cut-off. The guide's pass-time page gives the last runner as 07:15, but its own control table, the regs and the course page all say 06:30. An internal inconsistency in the guide — plan to the stricter 06:30 unless the briefing says otherwise, and confirm at bib pickup.

Food reserve. The guide's kit list specifies the reserve as minimum 500 calories — in the guide, that figure is the food-reserve spec. The regs page reads it as a separate reserve on top of the 500 kcal + 1 L start requirement. Carrying the larger interpretation can't hurt; the guide demands only the 500 kcal.

Bib position. The guide says bib on the front “at least at the control points”; the regs list a non-visible bib as a minor penalty with no such relaxation. Treat it as front-visible at all times.

Drop-bag point. The guide never names the 102K life-bag point; the regs say Confrides, 58.5 km. Not a contradiction, but unconfirmed — worth checking at pickup.

Crew-point shading. The guide's pass-time page fails to mark Font de Partagat (and Font del Pi's withdrawal-only status) in red, but its control table and crew list both include Partagat as a crew point — the shading is simply incomplete.

Spelling only. “Font del Alemany” vs “Font de l'Alemany”, and the guide's “Mallada del Llop” where the course page says “Mallà del Llop”.
